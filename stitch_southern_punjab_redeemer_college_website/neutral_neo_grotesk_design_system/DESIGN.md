# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a **Neutral / Modern Brutalist** hybrid aesthetic. It embraces a bold, high-contrast, yet restrained visual language that prioritizes absolute clarity and structural rigor. The emotional response is authoritative, sleek, and highly professional.

## Colors
- **Primary Color:** `#1a1a1a`
- **Secondary Color:** `#e63b2e`
- **Tertiary Color:** `#0055ff`
- **Neutral Color:** `#4a4a4a`

## Typography
- **Headlines & Labels:** Space Grotesk
- **Body:** Inter

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column fluid grid with strict spacing scales ensures reliable alignment and predictable component grouping.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through structural surface tiers and low-contrast borders rather than deep, diffused shadows.

## Shapes
Components feature soft, subtle rounding (`0.25rem` base radius) balancing geometric precision with approachability.

## Components
Includes standardized buttons, inputs, cards, and navigation primitives aligned with the neutral-modern aesthetic.
